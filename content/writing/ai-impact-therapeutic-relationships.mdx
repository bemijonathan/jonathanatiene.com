This research proposal examines the potential impacts of artificial intelligence integration in therapeutic settings, with a focus on how AI tools affect the development and maintenance of therapeutic relationships between practitioners and clients.

## Research Questions

The proposed study aims to address:

-   How do AI-assisted therapy tools influence client perceptions of therapeutic alliance?
-   What are the ethical considerations in balancing AI efficiency with human connection?
-   How can AI be optimally integrated to enhance rather than replace human therapeutic relationships?
-   What training do therapists need to effectively incorporate AI tools while maintaining strong therapeutic bonds?

## Methodology

The proposal outlines a mixed-methods approach combining quantitative measures of therapeutic outcomes with qualitative analysis of practitioner and client experiences with AI-augmented therapy sessions.
