The written internet is becoming statistically homogeneous. Here is why — and what we can do about it.

## The register is collapsing

Language models don't produce prose; they produce **the median of prose**. Trained on the aggregate of the written internet, they emit the mean.

<Callout tone="insight">
The interesting question is not whether AI writing is *good*. It is whether the space of possible writing is *collapsing*.
</Callout>

## What we can do

Write more, not less. Publish where language is still yours.
